# BIS Quality Control Orders (QCOs) — Mandatory Certification Reference
# Source: Official Gazette Notifications (gazetteofindia.gov.in) and BIS (bis.gov.in)
# This document covers major product categories under mandatory BIS QCOs.

---

## WHAT IS A QUALITY CONTROL ORDER (QCO)?

A Quality Control Order (QCO) is a statutory order issued by the Government of India under the BIS Act, 2016 (Section 16) or the Essential Commodities Act, 1955. Once a QCO is in force for a product:
- The product cannot be manufactured, imported, sold, or distributed in India without a valid BIS certification/registration
- Violations attract imprisonment up to 2 years and/or fine up to ₹2 lakh (first offence), higher penalties for repeat offences
- BIS conducts market surveillance to enforce compliance

## MAJOR ACTIVE QUALITY CONTROL ORDERS (As of 2024)

### CATEGORY: ELECTRONICS AND IT PRODUCTS (CRS)

**Order:** Electronics and IT Goods (Requirement for Compulsory Registration) Order, 2012 (as amended)
**Ministry:** Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY)
**Scheme:** CRS (Compulsory Registration Scheme)
**Portal:** crsbis.in

**Products Covered (Schedule I — Selected):**
- Mobile phones (IS 13252/IS 16046 as applicable)
- Laptops, notebooks, tablets (IS 13252 Part 1)
- LED lamps — self-ballasted (IS 15885 Part 2/Sec 13)
- LED luminaires (IS 10322 Part 5/Sec 3)
- Power banks (IS 16444)
- Set-top boxes (IS 16239)
- Wi-Fi routers (IS 13252 / applicable wireless standard)
- Smart meters (IS 15959)
- Printers (IS 13252)
- Microwave ovens (IS 302 Part 2/Sec 25)
- Electric vehicle supply equipment — AC (IS 17017)
- Lithium-ion batteries (IS 16046 / applicable)

**Compliance Path:** CRS Registration (self-declaration + BIS-recognised lab test report)

### CATEGORY: TOYS

**Order:** Toys (Quality Control) Order, 2020 (GSR 281(E) dated 21 May 2020, effective from 1 January 2021)
**Ministry:** Ministry of Commerce and Industry (DPIIT)
**Scheme:** Scheme I (ISI Mark)

**Products Covered:** All toys intended for children under 14 years, including:
- Electronic toys with batteries
- Stuffed/plush toys
- Puzzles and board games
- Outdoor play equipment (slides, swings — up to residential scale)
- Art and craft kits
- Baby rattles and teething toys

**Applicable Standards:**
- IS 9873 (Part 1):2021 — Mechanical and Physical Safety
- IS 9873 (Part 2):2021 — Flammability
- IS 9873 (Part 3):2021 — Chemical Properties (element migration)
- IS 9873 (Part 7):2021 — Finger paints

**Exemptions:**
- Sports equipment (separate standards)
- Christmas tree decorations
- Decorative articles not intended for children
- Puzzles with > 500 pieces (for adults)

**Import Compliance:** All toy imports must carry ISI Mark. No import clearance without BIS licence number or certificate of conformity.

### CATEGORY: PACKAGED DRINKING WATER

**Order:** Packaged Drinking Water (Quality Control) Order, 2000 (GSR 696(E))
**Ministry:** Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food and Public Distribution
**Scheme:** Scheme I (ISI Mark) — IS 14543:2016

**Key Requirements:**
- All packaged drinking water sold in India must carry ISI Mark
- Water source must be tested and approved
- Mandatory microbiological testing: absence of E.coli, coliform bacteria, Salmonella
- Chemical parameters per IS 10500:2012 limits
- Package sizes must match declared capacity (±5% tolerance)
- Label must show: IS 14543, BIS licence no., date of packaging, best-before date, batch number

### CATEGORY: GOLD JEWELLERY — HALLMARKING

**Order:** Gold (Compulsory Hallmarking) Order, 2021 (GSR 315(E) dated 6 May 2021)
**Ministry:** Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food and Public Distribution
**Scheme:** BIS Hallmarking Scheme

**Phased Implementation:**
- Phase 1 (June 16, 2021): 256 identified districts
- Phase 2 (April 1, 2023): Extended to all districts of India (with some artisan exemptions)

**Who Must Comply:**
- All gold jewellers selling gold jewellery/artefacts must be BIS-registered
- Every piece of gold jewellery sold must bear a BIS hallmark with HUID

**Exemptions:**
- Gold used in international trade (export-oriented units)
- Watches and spectacle frames
- Gold medals and coins from mints
- Gold jewellery of fineness not covered by IS 1417 (925, 800 grades)

### CATEGORY: MOTORCYLE HELMETS

**Order:** Protective Helmets for Two-Wheeler Riders (Quality Control) Order, 2020 (GSR 1(E) dated 1 January 2020)
**Ministry:** Ministry of Road Transport and Highways
**Scheme:** Scheme I (ISI Mark) — IS 16000:2012

**Requirements:**
- All helmets for sale in India must bear ISI Mark
- Both full-face and half-face helmets covered
- Helmet must withstand impact absorption test (≤ 400g), penetration test, and chin strap retention test
- Weight limit: ≤ 1.2 kg (full-face), ≤ 1.0 kg (half-face)
- Must have retroreflective material on left and right sides

## HOW TO VERIFY A QCO NOTIFICATION

1. Visit the Official Gazette of India: https://egazette.gov.in (search by Ministry or subject "Quality Control Order")
2. Visit BIS portal: https://bis.gov.in → "Quality Control Orders" section
3. Use the BIS Care app (Android/iOS) — provides searchable QCO list and product compliance check
4. Contact BIS Regional Office for your state/product category
