## SCHEME I — Product Certification (ISI Mark)

The ISI Mark is the most widely recognised BIS certification mark in India. It is granted under Scheme I of the BIS Act, 2016.

### Who Can Apply
- Indian manufacturers of products under mandatory Quality Control Orders (QCOs)
- Indian manufacturers seeking voluntary certification for any published Indian Standard
- Importers (through manufacturer agreement)

### Eligibility
- Manufacturer must have a manufacturing unit with adequate production and testing facilities
- Products must conform to the relevant Indian Standard

### Application Process
1. Submit Form I (Application for Grant of Licence) to the relevant BIS Regional/Branch Office
2. Pay the application fee (₹1,000 for small/micro enterprises; ₹10,000 for others — varies by order)
3. BIS evaluates the application and schedules an initial factory audit (Grant Inspection)
4. Samples drawn during factory audit are tested at BIS-recognised or NABL-accredited laboratory
5. If products conform to the standard and factory quality assurance is satisfactory, a BIS Licence is granted
6. Licensee can use the ISI Mark on certified product batches
7. Ongoing surveillance: BIS conducts random market surveillance inspections and annual factory inspections

### Key Documents Required
- Form I — Application for Grant of Licence
- Product test reports (valid for 6 months to 2 years depending on standard)
- Manufacturing process flowchart and Quality Assurance Plan (QAP)
- Site plan (factory layout)
- List of plant, machinery, and in-house test equipment
- Product-wise annual production capacity and turnover
- Certificate of incorporation / partnership deed / proprietorship details
- GST registration certificate
- List of products and applicable standard number(s)

### Fees
- Application processing fee: ₹1,000–₹10,000 (product/scheme specific)
- Annual Minimum Marking Fee (AMMF): Varies by product category; typically ₹3,000–₹1,00,000+ per year
- Testing charges: Borne by applicant at recognised laboratory

### Validity and Renewal
- BIS licence is valid for 1–3 years (product-specific)
- Renewal application to be submitted before expiry with updated test reports and surveillance record

## SCHEME II — Simplified Product Certification for Small-Scale Industries

A simplified certification scheme for small-scale manufacturers where the factory audit requirement is relaxed. Products are tested at a BIS-recognised external laboratory.

### Eligibility
- Manufacturers defined as Micro or Small Enterprises under MSME Act
- Products with relatively stable manufacturing processes

## FOREIGN MANUFACTURER CERTIFICATION SCHEME (FMCS)

Enables foreign manufacturers to obtain BIS licence for products that require mandatory ISI Mark to be imported and sold in India.

### Key Features
- No permanent Indian presence required (but an Authorised Indian Representative — AIR — is mandatory)
- Factory audit by BIS-empanelled foreign auditors (from the country of manufacture)
- Product testing at BIS-recognised laboratories or laboratories accredited by MRA (Mutual Recognition Agreement) partners: ILAC/APLAC, NABL (India), DAkkS (Germany), UKAS (UK), A2LA (USA), etc.

### Process
1. Apply to BIS (Headquarters, New Delhi) — Form FMCS-I
2. Pay application fee (USD 300)
3. Appoint an Authorised Indian Representative (AIR)
4. BIS empanelled auditor conducts factory audit in country of manufacture (cost borne by applicant)
5. Submit test reports from BIS-recognised / MRA-partner laboratory
6. Upon satisfactory audit and test reports, BIS licence granted
7. Annual surveillance fee: USD 500

### Required Documents
- FMCS-I application form
- Factory audit report (by BIS-empanelled auditor)
- Test reports from BIS-recognised or MRA-partner laboratory
- AIR appointment letter with authority and responsibility scope
- Manufacturing process documentation
- Quality control plan
- Sample submission (if required by BIS)

## COMPULSORY REGISTRATION SCHEME (CRS)

The CRS is a registration (not certification) scheme for electronics and IT goods under the Electronics and IT Goods (Requirement for Compulsory Registration) Order, 2012 (amended periodically).

### Covered Products (Schedule I of CRS Order — sample list)
- Mobile phones and smartphones
- Laptop and tablet computers
- LED lamps (self-ballasted)
- Power banks (lithium-ion/polymer)
- Electric vehicle chargers (Level 1/2)
- Printers, scanners, photocopiers
- Set-top boxes (DTH/cable)
- Smart meters
- Wi-Fi routers and modems
- UPS systems (< 10 kVA)
- LED luminaires (for indoor use)
- Microwave ovens
- Air conditioners (room type)

### Key Difference from ISI Mark (Scheme I)
- CRS is based on SELF-DECLARATION of conformity — no factory audit required
- Registration is per model (not per manufacturer as a whole)
- Test reports from BIS-recognised lab are the basis for registration
- Registration certificate issued by BIS bears a unique registration number
- Products must display: R-XXXXXX (registration number) on the device/packaging

### Process for Indian Manufacturers
1. Get product tested at a BIS-recognised laboratory for relevant IS (e.g., IS 13252 for laptops)
2. Submit online application on CRS portal (crsbis.in)
3. Upload test report, SDoC, product details
4. Pay registration fee (₹10,000 per application)
5. Receive Registration Certificate (R-No.) within 7 working days (if no discrepancy)

### Process for Foreign Manufacturers
1. Same as above but must appoint an Authorised Indian Representative (AIR)
2. Test report must be from a BIS-recognised laboratory

### CRS Registration Validity
- 2 years from date of issue (or expiry of test report, whichever is earlier)
- Renewal required before expiry with fresh test report if applicable standard has changed

## BIS HALLMARKING SCHEME

### Background
Gold hallmarking in India is governed by the BIS Act, 2016 and the Gold (Compulsory Hallmarking) Order, 2021 (GSR 315(E) dated 6 May 2021). As of 2023, mandatory hallmarking is enforced in 256+ notified districts.

### What is a Hallmark?
A hallmark on gold jewellery consists of FOUR components (mandatory since September 2021):
1. BIS Logo (triangle with 'BIS' inscription)
2. Purity/Fineness (e.g., 916 for 22-Karat gold = 91.6% gold)
3. AHC Code (Assaying and Hallmarking Centre code)
4. HUID (Hallmark Unique Identification) — a 6-character alphanumeric code unique to each jewellery piece

### Recognised Fineness Grades (IS 1417:2016)
| Karat | Fineness | % Gold |
|-------|---------|--------|
| 14K   | 585     | 58.5%  |
| 18K   | 750     | 75.0%  |
| 20K   | 833     | 83.3%  |
| 22K   | 916     | 91.6%  |
| 23K   | 958     | 95.8%  |
| 24K   | 999     | 99.9%  |

### Jeweller Registration
- All jewellers in notified districts MUST obtain a BIS licence before selling gold jewellery
- Application: Form B on BIS HUID Portal (huidonline.bis.gov.in)
- Fee: ₹5,000 registration; annual renewal ₹5,000
- Jeweller assigned a unique Jeweller ID on the BIS system

### HUID (Hallmark Unique Identification)
- Each piece of jewellery gets a unique 6-character alphanumeric HUID
- Assigned by the Assaying and Hallmarking Centre (A&H Centre)
- Consumers can verify HUID authenticity on the BIS Care app or bis.gov.in portal
- Hallmarking charge at A&H Centre: approximately ₹35 per piece (government-regulated)

### Who Cannot Hallmark
- Jewellers cannot hallmark in-house; must send all articles to a BIS-recognised A&H Centre
- As of 2024, there are 1500+ BIS-recognised A&H Centres across India

### Silver Hallmarking
- Silver hallmarking is currently voluntary (not mandatory) under BIS
- Governed by IS 2112:1989 — fineness grades: 800, 925, 999
- Procedure similar to gold; A&H Centres also handle silver

## MANAGEMENT SYSTEM CERTIFICATION (MSC)

BIS also offers certification for management systems under:
- IS/ISO 9001 — Quality Management Systems
- IS/ISO 14001 — Environmental Management Systems
- IS/ISO 22000 — Food Safety Management Systems
- IS/ISO 45001 — Occupational Health and Safety
- IS/ISO 27001 — Information Security Management

These are separate from product certification and are issued to organisations (not products).
