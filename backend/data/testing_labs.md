## BIS TESTING FRAMEWORK

### Who Can Test for BIS Certification?

**1. BIS-Recognised Laboratories**
Laboratories formally recognised by BIS for specific product categories. Their test reports are directly accepted for BIS licence applications. List maintained on bis.gov.in → "Recognised Laboratories".

**2. NABL-Accredited Laboratories (Schedule I labs)**
Laboratories accredited by National Accreditation Board for Testing and Calibration Laboratories (NABL) — India's accreditation body under the ILAC MRA. Test reports from NABL-accredited labs in relevant scope are accepted by BIS.

**3. MRA Partner Laboratory (for FMCS)**
For foreign manufacturers applying under FMCS, test reports from laboratories accredited by ILAC/APLAC MRA signatories are accepted (e.g., DAkkS Germany, UKAS UK, A2LA USA, JCSS Japan).

**4. BIS Own Laboratories**
BIS operates its own testing laboratories at Sahibabad (Central), and regional centres at Chennai, Kolkata, Chandigarh, Bengaluru, and Mumbai.

## COMMON BIS TESTING PROCEDURES BY CATEGORY

### A. Steel and Metals (IS 1786, IS 2062)
**Tests Required:**
1. Chemical composition analysis (C, Mn, S, P, Si, Cr, Ni by spectroscopy or wet analysis)
2. Tensile test (Yield Strength / 0.2% Proof Stress, UTS, elongation % on gauge length)
3. Bend test (bend 180° over specified mandrel without cracking)
4. Rebend test (bend to 45°, rest 30 min, straighten, rebend — no cracks)
5. Dimensions and mass per metre (for bars: diameter tolerance ±0.5mm for ≤10mm, ±0.6mm for >10mm)

**Sampling:** 1 sample per heat (or as specified in the standard)

### B. Cement (IS 8112, IS 1489)
**Tests Required (IS 4031 series):**
1. Fineness (Blaine air permeability) — min 225 m²/kg for OPC 43
2. Standard consistency (water content for normal consistency)
3. Setting time (Vicat needle: initial ≥ 30 min, final ≤ 600 min)
4. Soundness (Le Chatelier: expansion ≤ 10 mm; Autoclave: expansion ≤ 0.8%)
5. Compressive strength at 3, 7, and 28 days (70mm cube test)
6. Chemical analysis (CaO, SiO2, Al2O3, Fe2O3, MgO, SO3, Loss on Ignition)

**Sampling:** From lot — minimum 5 samples from different bags

### C. Toys (IS 9873)
**Tests Required:**
1. Mechanical and physical (Part 1): Small parts cylinder test, sharp edges/points test, cord/chain test, strangulation cord length (max 220mm continuous cord on toy), impact and drop test
2. Flammability (Part 2): Fabric burning rate test (horizontal/vertical), foam test
3. Chemical (Part 3): Element migration by HCl extraction + AAS/ICP analysis
4. Electrical (if battery-operated): IS 15998 / IEC 62115 compliance

**Age-Grade Labels:** Toys must clearly display age-grade (e.g., "Not suitable for children under 3 years")

### D. LED Lamps (IS 15885 — CRS)
**Tests Required:**
1. Cap dimensions and gauging (E27 or B22 base per IS 4109)
2. Safety at rated voltage and ±10% variation
3. Temperature test (Tcase at electrolytic capacitor: ≤ 105°C)
4. Insulation resistance (> 1 MΩ at 500 V DC for 1 min)
5. Electric strength test (2500 V AC for 1 min — no breakdown)
6. Photometric tests: Initial luminous flux, power factor, colour rendering index (CRI ≥ 70 recommended)
7. Lifetime (lumen maintenance at 2000 hours ≥ 95%)

### E. Packaged Drinking Water (IS 14543)
**Tests Required:**
1. Physical: Turbidity (< 1 NTU), Colour (< 5 Hazen units), Odour/Taste (unobjectionable)
2. Chemical: TDS (< 500 mg/l), pH (6.5–8.5), Hardness, Fluoride, Nitrate, Chloride, Sulphate, Heavy metals (Pb, As, Cd, Cr, Hg limits per IS 10500)
3. Microbiological: Total plate count (< 100 CFU/ml at 37°C), Coliforms (absent in 100 ml), E.coli (absent in 100 ml), Salmonella (absent), Yeast and Mould (absent)
4. Container tests: Burst test, drop test, leak test

**Frequency:** Every batch; microbiological tests every production shift recommended

### F. Solar PV Modules (IS 14286)
**Tests Required (IEC 61215 equivalent):**
1. Maximum power determination (STC: 1000 W/m², AM1.5, 25°C cell temp)
2. Insulation resistance test: > 400 MΩ·m² at 1000V DC
3. Thermal cycling: 200 cycles (−40°C to +85°C)
4. Humidity-freeze: 10 cycles (−40°C to +85°C, 85% RH)
5. Damp heat: 1000 hours at 85°C/85% RH
6. UV preconditioning: 15 kWh/m² UV dose
7. Mechanical load test: 2400 Pa (wind/snow)
8. Hail test: 25mm ice ball at 23 m/s, 11 impact locations
9. Bypass diode thermal test

## HOW TO FIND A BIS-RECOGNISED LABORATORY

**Step 1:** Visit bis.gov.in → Testing → Recognised Laboratories
**Step 2:** Filter by product category or IS number
**Step 3:** Cross-check NABL accreditation at nabl-india.org

**Tip:** For most products, NABL-accredited labs can be used in addition to BIS-recognised labs. Use NABL's directory search at nabl-india.org/search-accredited-lab.
